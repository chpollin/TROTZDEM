# Video formats

Choose the commissioned format and preserve accepted decisions in its project. The common procedure and verification stay in `SKILL.md`.

## Music

Derive the visual progression from the song’s particular conflict, voice and repetitions. An accepted text-free treatment remains text-free when adding images. Use the actual audio take as the time source. Separate vocals only when needed for the timing or reading task. Let the visual concept follow the commissioned interpretation, with or without displayed lyrics. When lyrics are shown, verify words and onsets independently. Text-free variants are a supported output, including when a dialect reading remains uncertain.

The local `hidden-by-the-grapes` project provides chalk with and without text, kinetic typography and linocut as distinct treatments. Its text-free `zweifel/` timeline records uncertain transcriptions without displaying guessed lyric lines. These are references, not a prescribed palette or mandatory typography.

## Explainer

Make the explanatory relationship observable through source-linked actions and legible holds. Keep the viewer’s question identifiable across transitions. Use a source-backed explanation and readable editorial durations. A silent video does not need vocal separation or transcription. The reference renderer expects `source/audio.wav`, so a silent track may be required by this implementation, not by the method itself. Match it to the verified scene length.

Keep the source image, published transcription, editorial expansion, norm-data match and own reading separate. A published expansion does not prove the shape of an abbreviation in the image. Put source references and unresolved readings in the project's source record. A local review can proceed while rights clearance for public use remains pending.

The local `urkunde-video/urkunde/` project demonstrates a facsimile embedded as a data URI, source-backed annotation and a silent track. Its open image-rights and abbreviation questions remain human review items.

## Promotional and brand video

Retain the commissioned tone and actual audience. A dramatic music-video treatment does not supply a default tone for factual promotion. Start from the accepted offer, audience, address and approved brand assets. Use a concrete promise supported by the offer and a claim that can be traced to evidence. Record whether a case is observed, anonymised, composite or invented, and label illustrative cases accordingly. Do not turn an illustrative before and after into a reported client result.

Distinguish a shorter cut or different musical arrangement from a series whose episodes do different work. A pending decision about series and real cases does not authorise producing them. Check every advertised deliverable against the accepted offer text, including the call to action.

Read the project's decisions before choosing sung advertising copy, instrumental music or speech. Synthetic speech and music require their own source, licence and suitability checks. Attribution should describe what was generated and what was rendered from code.

The local `dhcraft-promo/knowledge/advert-videos.md` records the operator's decisions for sober tone, du, a common brand line and attribution. The main and short spots use sung English copy, while the explainer and case story use instrumental beds. These choices remain project-specific. Check 16:9 and 1:1 separately, including attribution and the closing call to action. The existing centre square is a project layout decision, not a safe area for every possible aspect ratio.
