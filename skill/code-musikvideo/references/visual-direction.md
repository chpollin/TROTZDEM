# Visual direction

Use this reference for a new treatment or a material conceptual change. Reuse accepted direction for bounded revisions.

## Creative brief

Extract purpose, audience, central conflict or explanatory relationship, emotional movement, retained motifs and delivery constraints from the commission and source. Keep the brief in the project's existing production note. Mark reversible assumptions. Ask for information only where the answer changes a consequential choice. Genre adjectives alone do not determine imagery.

Distinguish a reference's transferable qualities from its literal subjects. A preferred engraving may establish materiality and line density without prescribing a chalkboard or historical setting. A supplied logo may require exact use. Record which properties the operator actually accepted.

## Translate content into actions

For each central passage, specify the intended meaning, a visible action and what the viewer can observe. An action changes a relationship, position, access, legibility, agency or state. Identify enough continuity for the change to be recognised. Choose the number of motifs and shots that the material needs.

Examples from Jagged Alien Intelligences are project-specific:

| Intended reading | Visible action | Continuity |
|---|---|---|
| Intellectual displacement | A human work area fills with a completed machine response | The same human caret and unfinished work remain identifiable |
| Defensive triumph | A missing letter count becomes an oversized human correction | The exact word stays readable before and after correction |
| Collective control | Separate agents coordinate and constrain the human's movement | The human marker stays recognisable until the elimination event |

An explainer may instead show evidence being linked to an annotation. A factual promotion may show an authorised document passing through a demonstrated workflow. Preserve the source and tone of that commission.

## Develop the visual progression

Name the specific properties that change across sections and what remains stable. Repetition may develop through coordination, depth, spatial restriction, deformation, scale, rhythm or silence. Select properties that carry the meaning. Increasing particle count alone does not establish intelligence or threat.

Distinguish the musical transition, visual change and intended reading. Some images need anticipation or a hold to be understood. Exact lyric timing does not require every visual event to start at the first syllable. Record editorial offsets in the timeline. A climax should change a state or relationship when the treatment calls for one.

## Review the treatment before expanding it

Use a small set of representative keyframes or a short sequence to inspect the main relationship and the strongest state change. Review recognisability, emotional tone, readable text and visual continuity. Expand once the treatment is coherent. Use targeted variants when there is a real open decision, and preserve accepted choices across later assets.

Inspect a sequence as motion as well as isolated images. A polished still cannot establish intelligible progression. For a comprehension review, ask what happened before revealing the intended explanation. Keep aesthetic preference, source accuracy and comprehension findings separate. Without an actual viewer response, describe comprehension as an intended reading.
