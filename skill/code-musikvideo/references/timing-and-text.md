# Timing and text

## Establish the selected source

Record the chosen take, its measured duration and the approved cut or full-recording decision. Resolve a requested duration ceiling against the actual file and current authorisation. Preserve an approved full recording's introduction and tail. Replacing, cutting or time-stretching audio requires renewed timing checks.

Keep author wording, an actual sung variant and an ASR reading distinct. A supplied lyric can be authoritative for a draft's display wording while its agreement with the recording remains unverified. State that limit beside the working timeline. Do not label author text as a listened transcription without hearing evidence.

## Use the available precision

Compare supplied text, free transcription, forced alignment and vocal phrase starts. A vocal rise supports an onset, not a word reading or a metrical downbeat. Zero-duration words, long stretches into pauses, repeated hooks and distorted voices need explicit inspection. Do not stretch failed intervals to invent word precision.

Use phrase- or line-level captions where finer timing is unsupported. Keep disputed variants and timing evidence with the affected passage. A lyrical spelling or stutter can be preserved as author wording without asserting each syllable has a measured onset. Present a short excerpt for human listening when that is the unresolved step.

Record measured anchors, inferred boundaries and editorial visual events separately. A ring appearing on a letter or an object rupturing may be an editorial choice near a sung phrase. Sound effects and musical downbeats need their own evidence before being described as measured synchronisation.

## Keep consumers on the maintained timeline

The corrected timeline is the maintained authority for text and timing used in the production. Derive scene captions, SRT/VTT, cue sheets, preview and companion-page cues from it where those deliverables are commissioned. Retain a mapping to source lines when several lines become one distorted-hook caption. Validate source coverage, order, positive intervals and recording bounds.

Inspect the actual downstream builder. A companion page that independently reads raw forced alignment can reintroduce a rejected onset even while the video and SRT are correct. Compare representative corrected passages across consumers. A shared filename does not establish shared timing. Adopt the smallest existing export path; avoid hand-maintained parallel timestamp lists.

## Review with the selected recording

Drive preview position from audio.currentTime. Explicit frame seeking is a separate render operation. Confirm seeking, pauses, end behaviour and visible failure when a required source is missing. Derived loudness or other analysis must be regenerated from the selected audio and loaded consistently by preview and renderer.

Inspect text at dense passages, repeats and the instrumental tail. Compare source, scene and stream durations with frame/codec rounding. Listen to difficult entries and representative transitions when possible. If listening is unavailable, identify the passages requiring it and retain the working status. Technical playback and ASR agreement do not establish perceptual sync.
