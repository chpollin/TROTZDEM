# CLAUDE.md

Project knowledge starts at `knowledge/INDEX.md`, open items are in `knowledge/handoff.md`, and the video production is described in `knowledge/video-pipeline-architecture.md`. Commands are in `video/README.md`.

## Rules

- A scene is a pure function `drawScene(ctx, t)`. All randomness is seeded, because frames are rendered out of order in parallel workers and must come out identical.
- Line onsets in `timeline.js` are checked against the free transcription and the vocal phrase starts in `source/timing-report.md`. Where the aligner and the phrase start disagree, the phrase start wins.
- Songs are staged without real brands or product UI.
- The videos carry no content warnings and no help or end cards. The operator removed them deliberately.
- `video/album.json` and `video/posters/` are generated by `tools/build_album.py` and not edited by hand. Page text lives in `index.html`, the per-video paragraph in `video/<slug>/making.txt`.
- Rendered videos never enter the repository. Web encodes go to the release `videos` with `gh release upload videos web/<slug>.mp4 --clobber`.
- Resource limits on the operator's workstation are one build agent at a time, `--workers 2`, stills in batches of at most eight, contact sheets at most 320 px per tile, and never two heavy commands in parallel. Several parallel render agents once nearly froze the machine.
- Page text is German and follows the operator's writing style. Code, comments, commit messages and `knowledge/` are English.
- `source/`, `out/`, `web/` and `.venv/` are not versioned.
