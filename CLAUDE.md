# CLAUDE.md

Project knowledge starts at `knowledge/INDEX.md`, the framing of the project is in `knowledge/project.md`, open items are in `knowledge/handoff.md`, and the video production is described in `knowledge/video-pipeline-architecture.md`. Commands are in `video/README.md`.

## Rules

- A scene is a pure function `drawScene(ctx, t)`. All randomness is seeded, because frames are rendered out of order in parallel workers and must come out identical.
- Line onsets in `timeline.js` are checked against the free transcription and the vocal phrase starts in `source/timing-report.md`. Where the aligner and the phrase start disagree, the phrase start wins.
- TROTZDEM is presented as an experiment with generative AI, never as personal songs. Every public text follows `knowledge/project.md`.
- Songs are staged without real brands or product UI.
- A deliberately provocative song text gets a context note as `note` in `video/songs.json`.
- The videos carry no content warnings and no help or end cards. The operator removed them deliberately.
- `video/album.json`, `video/posters/` and `video/live/` are generated by `tools/build_album.py` and not edited by hand. Page text lives in `index.html`, the per-video paragraph in `video/<slug>/making.txt`.
- Rendered videos never enter the repository. Web encodes go to the release `videos` with `gh release upload videos web/<slug>.mp4 --clobber`.
- Resource limits on the operator's workstation are `--workers 2` per agent, stills in small batches, contact sheets at most 320 px per tile, a full render only when no other ffmpeg process runs, and never two heavy commands in parallel. About eight parallel renders once nearly froze the machine.
- Page text is German and follows the operator's writing style. Code, comments, commit messages and `knowledge/` are English.
- `source/`, `out/`, `web/` and `.venv/` are not versioned.
